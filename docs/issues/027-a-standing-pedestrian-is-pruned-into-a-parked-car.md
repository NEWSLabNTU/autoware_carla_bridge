# 027 — A pedestrian standing near a parked car flickers out of the tracked objects

**Severity**: High
**Component**: `multi_object_tracker` parameters (`pruning_generalized_iou_thresholds`),
`src/acb_launch/launch/components/tier4_perception_component.launch.xml`,
`scripts/sync_perception_component.sh`
**Status**: Fixed (tracker's UNKNOWN pruning GIoU 0.1, as upstream)

## What happened

carla-scenario-bridge roadmap 033, incident 2 (`town01_i2_ped_dart.xosc`): a pedestrian
steps out from in front of a parked car into the ego's lane and stands there 4 s. In run
`d30_2` (2026-10-17) the ego slowed to 2.8 m/s, then accelerated into the standing
pedestrian. `/perception/object_recognition/objects` reported it only intermittently
between 10 and 4 m: present 877.88, gone 878.48-879.08, back 879.38. 4 of 5 runs passed.

## Where it is dropped (live run `base2`, 2026-10-18, stock stack, clustering)

Per message, while the walker stands in the lane 2-30 m ahead of the ego, is the walker
there (points within 0.8 m of its CARLA position, or an object within 1.5 m)?

| Stage | Frames with the walker |
|-------|------------------------|
| `/sensing/lidar/concatenated/pointcloud` | 93/93 (mean 544 points) |
| `obstacle_segmentation/pointcloud` (ground removed) | 93/93 (269) |

So CARLA's lidar sees the walker in every frame, and every detection stage keeps it; the
tracker loses it. Not the sensor config, not ground segmentation, not the cluster size,
not the validator.

## Why: overlap pruning between two UNKNOWNs

`multi_object_tracker` (Autoware 0.48.0, `processor.cpp`, `mergeOverlappedTracker`) prunes
"duplicate" trackers every cycle. For two trackers that are both UNKNOWN -- everything is,
under `lidar_detection_model: clustering` -- within `pruning_distance_thresholds[UNKNOWN]`
= 9 m of each other, it merges one into the other when their generalized IoU exceeds
`pruning_generalized_iou_thresholds[UNKNOWN]` = **-0.3**, removing the one with the larger
position covariance. GIoU is `IoU - (hull - union) / hull` over the two footprints' convex
hull, so a negative threshold admits pairs that do not overlap at all.

The walker stands 4.9 m from the parked car's centre (1.4 m from its front corner). The
car's tracker is old and settled; the walker's is young, so it is the one removed. GIoU of
the car's track and the walker's detection, computed as the tracker does, against whether
the walker's track was published:

```
t=439029.05 GIoU=-0.29  walker track Y    (a fresh tracker, just confirmed)
t=439029.15 GIoU=-0.28  -                 (merged into the car)
...         -0.28..-0.30  -  for 1.2 s
t=439030.25 GIoU=-0.37  Y                 (cluster shapes moved; under -0.3 again)
...         -0.34..-0.37  Y
t=439030.75 GIoU=-0.29  -
```

Across the window the walker's track is present when GIoU < -0.30 and absent when it is
above, frame by frame. Each removal spawns a new tracker from the next detection, which is
published once confirmed and pruned again at the next cycle above the threshold: the
flicker. It needs both trackers UNKNOWN and one of them settled, which a parked car and a
pedestrian standing beside it are; while the walker walks, its track follows its
detection (79 % against 85 % of frames in `base2`).

Why the ego then drives on: `obstacle_stop` does not stop for the obstacle pointcloud inside
the path (`obstacle_filtering.check_inside.pointcloud: false`), only for objects, and holds
a vanished stop obstacle for 1 s (`stop_obstacle_hold_time_threshold`, `min_off_duration`).
A gap over ~1 s releases the stop.

## Upstream

autoware_launch moved this threshold: per-label `-0.3` for UNKNOWN until 2026-06-15, then
`0.1` for every label (#1885, 2026-06-16, synced from autoware_universe), then replaced by
`pruning_unknown_pair_min_giou: 0.1` plus `pruning_unknown_pair_max_gap: 1.0` m (#1907,
2026-07-21) -- "negative admits disjoint pairs". Autoware 1.5.0 predates all of it. The
0.48.0 code reads the per-label array, so the fix is a parameter, not code.

## Fix

acb_launch's perception component now passes acb's copy of
`multi_object_tracker_node.param.yaml`
(`config/perception/object_recognition/tracking/multi_object_tracker/`), upstream's but for
`pruning_generalized_iou_thresholds[UNKNOWN]`: **0.1** instead of -0.3, upstream's own later
value. Two UNKNOWNs are merged only when they overlap. Both files are generated by
`scripts/sync_perception_component.sh` (`--check` after an Autoware upgrade); three tests in
`src/acb_bridge/tests/launch_profile.rs` hold the value, the copy against upstream, and the
component's path. Nothing in Autoware's code changes, so no overlay (csb roadmap 042).

Left alone: `obstacle_stop` still ignores the obstacle pointcloud inside the path. Turning
`check_inside.pointcloud` on would stop the ego for every obstacle point in its path, a
behaviour change for every scenario that wants its own testing; with the object steady it
is not needed here.
